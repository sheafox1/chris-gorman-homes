How It Works

How building with us works

From the first call to the keys.

1. Property, plan and price

Start with the big picture: your property, your priorities and your budget. We look at all three together.

2. Design with an architect

You do not need a finished floor plan. Inspiration photos and examples from homes you have visited are enough to begin.

3. Selections and budget

Choose materials, fixtures and finishes while we gather pricing and keep selections connected to the overall budget.

4. Finance and prepare

We coordinate financing, permits, scheduling and preconstruction before the build begins.
